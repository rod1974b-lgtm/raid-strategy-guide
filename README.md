# Raid Strategy Guide

I want to make the ultimate Raid Shadow Legends tactical companion to help me progress through the game in real time.

My biggest issue right now is that I'm mid-late game. I have a lot of champions and gear, but no easy way to gear the champions according to the boss. Every boss and level are different. I need an easy way to pull gear from not used champions and suggest gear sets from all champions. Look at all my gear and suggest what to build 1st to achieve the stats needed to beat each stage.

I'd like a versatile core team if possible, and guidance on who next to 60, 50, 40 and who to fully book skills. I need an easier way to strip gear from champions on the fly and have step by step coaching for any part and level of the game: Clan Boss, Chimera, Hydra, Doom Tower, Grim Forest, Faction Wars, Campaign, Sintranos / Amius, Iron Twins, Sand Devil, Shogun's Grove, Dungeons (Normal & Hard), Minotaur, Potion Keeps, Classic Arena, Tag Team Arena (3v3), Live Arena, Clan Siege, and CvC.

Provide wave-survival and boss strategies, exact stage stat checkpoints (True Speed, ACC, RES, EHP), turn-order sequencing, and Auto AI skill presets. Please implement this using the attached comprehensive master plan starting with Phase 1.
